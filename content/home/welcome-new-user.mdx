# Welcome to the HPV Vaccine Communication Training!

As a clinician, you understand the importance of the HPV vaccine — but parents of your patients may not. This training will help you overcome common objections using the CLEAR method, then you'll put those skills to practice through a realistic AI simulation.

In the simulation, an AI parent avatar will play the role of a hesitant parent, responding realistically as you work through the conversation. First, we'll guide you though using the CLEAR method, and then you will get to practice a full conversation with a virtual parent. Let's get started!
